# Drill Snake generated art prompts

The prototype textures and sprite atlases below were created with the built-in
OpenAI ImageGen tool on July 28, 2026. The supplied industrial mining-game
screenshot was included as the visual reference for every prompt.

## Mine floor

> Use case: stylized-concept  
> Asset type: seamless Unity game material texture for a top-down mining floor  
> Primary request: Create a square, perfectly tileable hand-painted dark slate
> mine-floor texture matching the visual language of the provided reference
> image.  
> Scene/backdrop: only a flat orthographic floor surface, no walls and no
> perspective.  
> Style/medium: polished 2D game art, chunky painterly forms, readable at
> gameplay scale.  
> Composition/framing: 8-by-8 large square stone/concrete floor slabs filling
> the entire image; seamless on all four edges.  
> Lighting/mood: neutral low-contrast ambient lighting baked very subtly; no
> directional spotlight or vignette.  
> Color palette: charcoal gray, desaturated blue-gray, muted warm gray grout.  
> Materials/textures: worn slabs, hairline cracks, chipped corners, faint dust
> and small embedded pebbles; restrained detail, strong tile readability.  
> Constraints: square texture, top-down orthographic, seamless/tileable, uniform
> scale, no objects, no ore, no machinery, no text, no UI, no borders, no
> watermark.  
> Avoid: cave walls, perspective, dramatic shadows, bright highlights, random
> noisy speckling, photorealism.
